About Our System

LifeLink is a digital Blood Donation Management and Registry System built to close the gap between willing donors and patients in urgent need. Donors register their blood type and location, hospitals search verified donor records in seconds, and blood banks track inventory in real time — all on one secure platform.

  • ✔ Fast donor-to-hospital matching by blood group & location
  • ✔ Real-time inventory tracking for blood banks
  • ✔ Secure, verified donor and recipient records
  • ✔ Urgent request alerts for critical shortages
